Climate zones›Central units› New central unit

1 · What is it?

A central unit is equipment that several room units depend on. Home Assistant usually sees it as just a switch, or not at all.

2 · How does HZT turn it on?

HZT will own this switch. Turning it on or off elsewhere shows as a manual override on the dashboard.

3 · Which radiators does it feed?

Home Assistant doesn’t know your pipework. Tick every radiator on this boiler’s circuit.

4 · When to fire

Fire when room or more is calling for its radiator. Keep at least valve open while firing.
